## Key Metrics for Forecast Error
- **MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error)**: 
  `AVG(ABS(actual - forecast) / NULLIF(ABS(actual), 0)) * 100`
 
- **RMSE (Root Mean Square Error)**:
  `SQRT(AVG(POWER(actual - forecast, 2)))`

- **MAE (Mean Absolute Error)**:
  `AVG(ABS(actual - forecast))`

- **WMAPE (Weighted MAPE)**:
  `SUM(ABS(actual - forecast)) / SUM(ABS(actual)) * 100`
